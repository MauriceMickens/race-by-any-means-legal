Privacy Policy

Overview

Race By Any Means uses the information you provide to create training plans, explain workouts, track progress, and improve the guidance shown to you. We do not sell your personal data, show targeted advertising, or use your data to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Information we collect

  • Account information. If you choose Sign in with Apple, we may receive your name, email address, and Apple account identifier. You can also use the app through an anonymous session.
  • Profile and training information. This may include age, sex, height, weight, goals, schedule, available equipment, injuries or limitations, and other notes you provide.
  • Fitness and performance information. This may include race results and splits, training plans, workout logs, pace and completion data, readiness check-ins, fatigue, pain feedback, and related coaching responses.
  • User content. This may include race-result photos and free-form text you submit for analysis or coaching.
  • Identifiers. We use account, athlete, and app-generated device identifiers to keep your session working and associate your data with the correct profile. We do not use the Apple advertising identifier for tracking.

Location and run tracking

If you enable run tracking, the app may use precise location on your device to measure the run. Detailed route points remain on the device. The service receives aggregate workout information, such as duration and completed work, rather than your precise route.

How we use information

  • Authenticate you or maintain an anonymous session.
  • Create and personalize training plans and coaching.
  • Read race results and summarize performance.
  • Sync plans, workouts, check-ins, and progress across sessions.
  • Operate, secure, troubleshoot, and support the service.

Your permission for AI data sharing

Our AI provider is OpenAI. We use its API to interpret race-result images, analyze race performance, generate and personalize training plans, and write workout and daily coaching explanations. Before these features send your personal information to OpenAI, the app shows a separate AI data-sharing disclosure and asks for your affirmative permission. Agreeing to exercise risks or the Terms of Use is not permission for AI data sharing.

Depending on the feature, the information sent to OpenAI may include:

  • Training answers and constraints: name, age, training background, goals, available days and time, equipment, and injury areas, severity, limitations or exercise restrictions you provide.
  • Race information: dates, event locations, division, results, running and station splits, transition times, race notes, upcoming race details and time goals. An analysis may use previous race results as well as the result you are viewing.
  • Training and coaching context: planned workouts, training phase, recent workout dates and performance summaries, completed work, duration, repetitions and sets, run-distance context, fatigue, feedback, and relevant check-in or context-event summaries, including health or recovery information you choose to record.
  • Uploaded race-result images: the complete image you choose to upload. Images and free-form notes or feedback may contain names or other personal information. We do not promise that these are automatically removed before processing. Crop or remove anything you do not want analyzed before uploading.

We send the context needed for the particular feature; not every stored profile field is included in every AI request. Our AI integration does not intentionally send sign-in passwords or authentication tokens, payment details, or precise run-route points. Do not put those details in images, race notes or feedback.

You may choose Don't allow on the consent screen. AI features remain unavailable until you give permission; declining does not delete your account or grant permission in the background. The app records the accepted disclosure and privacy-policy versions, the account that accepted them, and a server timestamp. Existing users must accept the new AI disclosure, rather than having an older Terms acceptance treated as permission. We will request renewed permission before sharing with a different AI provider or under a materially changed disclosure.

Service providers and protection

We use service providers only to operate app features:

  • OpenAI receives the information described above to operate the AI features you permit.
  • Supabase supports authentication and stores app data used to provide your profile, plans, and workout history.
  • Railway hosts the app's API service.
  • Apple provides optional Sign in with Apple, push-notification delivery and the App Store. If you enable personalized notification previews, race or coaching details may be visible on your lock screen.

We require providers receiving personal information to provide the same or equal protection described in this policy, including confidentiality, appropriate security, limited use for providing the service, and applicable deletion and retention obligations. Their processing is governed by the relevant service and data-protection agreements, not permission to sell your information or use it for targeted advertising.

OpenAI documents that API data is not used for model training by default; training requires a separate opt-in by the API account owner. API processing is different from using a consumer chatbot. See OpenAI's API data controls and retention documentation for its processing safeguards and exceptions. Your permission here is for the app features described above, not a request to contribute your data to model training.

Race-result images

After AI-sharing permission is recorded, a race-result image is sent to OpenAI when you choose to upload it. Race By Any Means does not store the original image in its application database. The extracted race information may be saved to your profile and used to personalize training. OpenAI's processing and retention rules still apply to the submitted image.

Retention and security

We retain profile, plan, race, and workout information while it is needed to operate the service, maintain your history, meet legal obligations, and resolve security or support issues. Service providers may retain request data under their own terms. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

OpenAI may retain API content and metadata for abuse monitoring and feature operation. Standard abuse-monitoring retention is up to 30 days, with legal, safety and other documented exceptions. We do not promise zero retention or that stopping use of a feature recalls information already processed.

Your choices

  • Sign in with Apple is optional.
  • You can deny or revoke photo and location permissions in iOS Settings.
  • You can avoid uploading race-result images and enter results manually.
  • You can decline AI sharing before using AI features. Manual race entry avoids sending an image, but any later AI analysis of the entered results still requires AI-sharing permission.
  • To stop future AI use, stop using AI features and contact support to request withdrawal or deletion. Changing photo or location permissions alone does not withdraw AI-sharing permission. A dedicated in-app AI-sharing withdrawal toggle is not currently available.
  • You can request access to or deletion of account data through the support page.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The latest version and effective date will remain available on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, visit Support. Do not include health, account, or other sensitive information in a public support request. We will arrange a private follow-up when needed.
