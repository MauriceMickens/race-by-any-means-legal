Open a support request

GitHub support requests are public. Do not include health information, account details, race-result images, or other sensitive information. We will arrange a private follow-up when needed.

Before opening a request

  1. Confirm you are using the newest TestFlight or App Store version.
  2. Close and reopen the app.
  3. Check that your device has an internet connection.
  4. Include the app version, device model, and a short description of what happened.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Sign in with Apple is optional. You can complete onboarding and use the app through an anonymous session.

Can I enter a race result without uploading a photo?

Yes. Choose manual entry during onboarding or when adding a race result.

Why does the app ask for location?

Location is used only when you choose automatic outdoor run tracking. Detailed route points remain on your device.

Is the coaching medical advice?

No. The app provides fitness and training guidance only. Stop for pain or concerning symptoms and seek qualified medical help when appropriate.
